## Product quality, textures, closures and lace

### Raw, virgin and Remy hair: what is the difference

Virgin hair is 100% unprocessed human hair that has never been chemically treated, dyed or bleached. Raw hair is usually from a single donor, only cleaned, with the cuticles intact and aligned; it is the highest quality and lasts longest. Remy means all the cuticles face the same direction, which greatly reduces tangling and matting. Ivycandy Hair specialises in premium virgin and raw human hair.

### Is the 8A, 9A, 10A grade system real

No. The letter grading system (8A, 9A, 10A and so on) is mostly a marketing tool created by suppliers. What matters is whether the hair is truly virgin or raw, from a single donor, and has aligned cuticles, not the number.

### Is all the hair 100% human hair

Yes. Ivycandy Hair only sells 100% human hair. It is never blended with synthetic fibres.

### Will the hair look exactly like the photos

The photos give a good idea of texture and length. Because raw and virgin hair comes from real donors, slight natural differences in texture or colour between bundles are normal, and they help create a more realistic look once installed.

### Which hair textures are available

### Why curly or wavy hair looks shorter than the length ordered

Hair is measured when pulled straight and taut. Once it returns to its natural curl or wave pattern it looks about 2 to 6 inches shorter, depending on the texture. For curly and wavy textures, order 2 to 4 inches longer than the length you want to wear.

### Can the hair be dyed or bleached

Yes, it is 100% human hair, so it can be dyed or bleached. A professional stylist is strongly recommended, especially for bleaching, because chemical processing can affect the hair's texture and how long it lasts. Hair that has been coloured, bleached or chemically treated may not qualify for a voluntary return (see the returns policy).

### What is the natural colour of the hair

Most virgin and raw hair is natural black (1 or 1B) or a very dark brown. Slight differences between bundles are normal.

### Can the weft be cut

Machine wefts can usually be cut without unravelling, but cutting is not recommended unless necessary, because it can increase shedding at the cut edge. Cut or altered hair may not qualify for a voluntary return (see the returns policy).

### Closure or frontal: what is the difference

A closure (usually 4x4, 5x5 or 6x6) covers a smaller leave-out area at the crown or part. A frontal (13x4 or 13x6) runs from ear to ear and gives more styling flexibility: middle, side or free parts.

### What is HD lace or transparent lace

HD (high definition) and transparent lace blend more naturally with a wide range of skin tones and are less visible than traditional lace.

## How many bundles do I need

### How many bundles are needed for a full sew-in

A general guide with 100g bundles: 10 to 16 inches needs 2 to 3 bundles; 18 to 22 inches needs 3 bundles; 24 to 28 inches needs 3 to 4 bundles; 30 inches and longer needs 4 or more. For extra fullness, or if your natural hair is fine, add one extra bundle.

### Are 3 bundles enough

Yes for most styles up to 24 inches. For 26 inches and longer, or for a very full look, plan on 4 bundles.

### Are bundles still needed with a closure or frontal

Yes. A closure or frontal covers only the leave-out area. It does not replace the bundles needed for volume and length.

### How much does one bundle weigh

A standard bundle weighs about 100 grams (3.5 oz). Longer lengths can look thinner because the same weight is spread over more length.

### Mixing different lengths in one install

When bundles of different lengths are mixed, count the number of bundles by the longest length. Plan the bundle count for that length (see how many bundles are needed).

## Care, longevity and styling

### How long the hair lasts

With proper care a sew-in install lasts 4 to 8 weeks. The hair itself can be reused for 1 to 3 years or more; raw and virgin hair often lasts longest.

### Does the hair shed

A little shedding is normal with any wefted hair, especially in the first few washes. Heavy shedding usually comes from improper care or rough handling. If the hair sheds heavily from the moment it is delivered, contact support with photos; it is handled as a possible defective product. ### How to wash and care for the hair

Use sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner and wash 1 to 2 times a week, or as needed. Deep condition regularly. Detangle gently from the ends to the roots with a wide-tooth comb or your fingers while the hair is wet and conditioned. Air dry or use low heat. Sleep on a satin or silk pillowcase or in a bonnet. Avoid heavy oils and products that cause build-up.

### Can the hair be straightened, curled or blow-dried

Yes. Use a heat protectant and keep the temperature moderate: avoid heat above 350°F (177°C).

### How long does a sew-in last

A sew-in typically lasts 4 to 8 weeks, depending on how fast your natural hair grows and how well you maintain it.

### Can the hair be reused after taking it down

Yes, if it is removed carefully and properly cleaned and conditioned.

### Is the hair good for wigs, glueless installs and ponytails

Yes. The bundles work for sew-ins, custom wigs, glueless installs, ponytails and other styles.

### Common buying mistakes to avoid

Common mistakes: buying too few bundles for the length or fullness wanted; ordering curly or wavy hair at exactly the worn length you want (it will look shorter); using harsh shampoos or skipping deep conditioning; sleeping without satin protection; and bleaching or heavily processing the hair without a professional.
